---

## 1. Product Summary
Fast, accurate, 100% private client-side financial calculators. Runs entirely inside the user's browser via JavaScript; zero personal financial numbers or queries are transmitted or stored on servers. Free, zero sign-up required, zero ad spam.

- **Primary Offering:** 38+ precision financial calculation tools across mortgages, loans, compound interest, retirement planning (401k, IRA, FIRE, Social Security), taxes, and hardware emulators (HP 12C, TI BA II Plus).
- **Core Value Proposition:** Absolute privacy (client-side math), instantaneous computation, multi-currency conversion (USD, EUR, GBP, JPY, CAD, AUD, CHF, BRL, INR, MXN), and 6-language internationalization (`en`, `es`, `de`, `ja`, `fr`, `pt`).
- **Conversion Goal:** Tool usage, recurring return visits, bookmarking, PWA installs, and newsletter/feedback engagement.

---

## 2. Competitive Landscape
Direct search competitors ranking on Google Page 1:

1. **Calculator.net** (`https://www.calculator.net/financial-calculator.html`): Dominates general search volume. Strengths: High domain authority (DR 85+), thousands of pages. Weaknesses: Cluttered UI, outdated design, not privacy-branded.
2. **fncalculator.com** (`https://www.fncalculator.com/`): Established classic financial calculator portal. Strengths: Brand recognition. Weaknesses: 2010-era design, poor mobile experience, slow rendering.
3. **Bankrate.com** (`https://www.bankrate.com/calculators/`): Dominates high-commercial loan and mortgage queries. Weaknesses: Heavy lead-generation forms asking for phone numbers and emails to sell to mortgage brokers.
4. **MidhaFin / HP12C.pro**: Specialized emulators for CFA and RPN. Weaknesses: Limited feature set, non-responsive mobile UI.

---

## 3. Core Differentiation & "Why We Win"
1. **Privacy-First (No Data Harvesting):** Unlike Bankrate or loan aggregators, we never gate calculators behind email captures or sell user data to lenders.
2. **Hardware Emulators:** Full RPN emulation with keyboard shortcuts for HP 12C and TI BA II Plus.
3. **Modern Vercel-Style Aesthetic:** Clean typography (Geist Mono, Plus Jakarta Sans), dark mode, smooth charts, and responsive mobile controls.
4. **Complete Internationalization:** Native localized calculators and hreflang tags for Spanish, German, Japanese, French, and Portuguese.
